# Start Here — Inbox Zero

You have a zip. Here's the one thing to do: get the file **`installer.md`** in
front of your AI (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or a coding terminal). Find your
device below.

## iPhone / iPad

1. Open the **Files** app and find the zip you downloaded.
2. **Tap the zip** — it unzips into a folder with the same name.
3. Open that folder and tap **`installer.md`**.
4. Tap the **Share** button and share it into your **Claude** or **ChatGPT**
   app — or open the file, select all, copy, and paste it into the AI chat.

## Windows

1. **Right-click** the zip → **Extract All** → **Extract**.
2. Open the folder that appears.
3. **Drag `installer.md`** into your AI app or the AI website's chat box — or
   open it in Notepad, select all, copy, and paste it into the chat.

## Mac

1. **Double-click** the zip — it unzips into a folder next to it.
2. Open the folder.
3. **Drag `installer.md`** into your AI app or the AI website's chat box — or
   open it, copy everything, and paste it into the chat.

---

Send that file to your AI and say: **install inbox zero.**
