## Why each section exists

**What I did** — counts, not a list. A digest that reprints the inbox is the
inbox.

**What needs you** — the flagged items only. Three, not eleven. A flag list
longer than a person can act on in a morning is not a flag list; the skill caps
it at eight and says so when it caps.

**I wasn't sure about these** — the section a sorter that wanted to look good
would leave out. It is what trains the folder list, and it is never omitted:
when it is genuinely empty it says "nothing." A sorter hiding its own error rate
is one you stop being able to correct.

**Anything suspicious** — quoted verbatim, with what was NOT done. This section
is omitted entirely when nothing suspicious appeared, rather than printing a
reassuring "no threats detected" line every morning that nobody reads.

**Receipt** — two numbers that have to match. If they had not, there would be no
digest claiming a clean run.
