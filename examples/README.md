# Worked examples — see the whole flow before you point it at your inbox

Five complete artifacts from one made-up business, end to end: the messy inbox
that went in, the folder list it was sorted against, the digest that came out,
the voice profile the kit wrote, and one draft reply with the ledger row that
receipted it.

Nothing is trimmed to look good. The refusals are in here too, because that is
half of what you are buying — a sorter that always produces an answer is not
making judgments.

| File | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| `01-morning-sweep-transcript.md` | A full morning sweep on a 14-message inbox. Classification into a closed folder list, three things flagged and the rest deliberately not, two refusals (an unreadable message, a folder that does not exist), one email trying to give the AI orders, and the actions-versus-ledger-rows count. |
| `02-taxonomy.md` | The filled folder list this business ended up with after the install interview — seven folders, each with a meaning, and the fallback folder named. |
| `03-daily-digest.md` | The digest that sweep produced. Five sections including the "I wasn't sure" section and the suspicious-message section. |
| `04-voice-profile-external.md` | The style document the kit wrote for this owner's outside-the-company voice, after their redaction pass. Patterns only — no quotes, no names, no client details. Read what it does NOT contain. |
| `05-draft-reply-with-ledger-row.md` | One reply drafted end to end: the missing-fact gate stopping twice (once for a number, then again when the answer came back as a range), the finished draft, where it landed, and the ledger row that recorded which voice it used. |

## Everything in here is made up

**The business, the people, the companies, the addresses, and the numbers are
all invented for the demonstration.** Every email domain is `example.com`, which
is a domain reserved for exactly this purpose and reaches nobody. No real
inbox, no real client, and no real correspondence appears anywhere in these
files.

The *shapes* are not invented — a chased invoice, a supplier backorder, a
newsletter pile, and one message written to manipulate whatever reads it are
what an actual small-business inbox looks like on a Tuesday.

## These examples are never your verification

The kit's install is not complete until it has run on **your own mail**. Nothing
in this folder can stand in for that, and the kit is written to refuse if you
try: if a sweep returns nothing real, it stops and asks you to paste one thread
rather than acting out a demonstration.

When you are ready, open your mail and say **"morning sweep."** The flow below
is exactly what runs on yours — same folders, same flag criteria, same gates,
your correspondents and your numbers.
